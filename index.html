Safety checklist before attempting a project

Use this as a starting point—not a complete engineering or installation checklist.

  • Check local electrical, building and fire codes; obtain permits where required.
  • Have system sizing, protection, grounding and wiring reviewed by a qualified electrician or engineer.
  • Use batteries, chargers and inverters that are compatible and approved for the intended application.
  • Plan for overcurrent protection, ventilation, temperature, physical protection and emergency shutdown.
  • Never improvise connections to household wiring or the utility grid.
  • Follow manufacturer instructions and applicable safety standards.
